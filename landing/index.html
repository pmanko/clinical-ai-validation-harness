About the initiative

About Open Clinical AI

Open Clinical AI explores practical uses of open-weight models in clinical and health-system workflows, including deployments on local infrastructure.

Experimental software. Outputs require review. Demonstrations and research results do not establish clinical safety or effectiveness.
