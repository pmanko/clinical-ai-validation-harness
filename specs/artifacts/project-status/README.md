# Project evidence inventory

This inventory contains dated implementation, review, pull-request and publication
observations. Check each row's evidence date. Recorded next actions and acceptance
notes describe that checkpoint; they do not schedule current work or establish
current PR, deployment or owner-acceptance status.

Use the [OpenClinAI roadmap](https://github.com/pmanko/openclinai.org/blob/main/specs/roadmap.md)
for current coordination, the [Catalyst delivery section](https://github.com/pmanko/openclinai.org/blob/main/specs/roadmap.md#6-catalyst-delivery)
for cross-project acceptance, and the product authorities they link for behavior.
Harness experiments remain in [Feature 006](../../006-validation-harness-mvp/spec.md),
[Feature 008](../../008-catalyst-query-workbench/spec.md) and the
[Catalyst comparison protocol](../../catalyst-program-roadmap.md).

| Evidence | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Pull requests](pull-requests.md) | Recorded heads, check signals and dispositions. Refresh GitHub before acting. |
| [Efforts](efforts.md) | Dated checkpoints and their then-recorded next actions. |
| [Roadmaps](roadmaps.md) and [artifacts](artifacts.md) | Requirement owners and evidence references, with individual dates. |
| [Public surfaces](public-surfaces.md) and [reports](reports.md) | Publication observations, reports and their claim limits. |
| [Decisions](decisions.md) and [sessions](sessions.md) | Supporting records, not current requirements. |
| [September 12 review](reviews/2026-09-12.md) and [September 6 report](reports/2026-09-06.md) | Dated reconnaissance and review evidence. |
| [Maintenance](maintenance.md) | JSON inputs and generated Markdown/CSV views. |

Keep dated reviews identifiable as evidence. Current requirements belong in their
maintained owners, not copied into this inventory. Missing CI is unknown, and an
old passing check or merge does not establish deployed acceptance. Raw screenshots
and recordings remain outside Git. Publication belongs to the umbrella.
